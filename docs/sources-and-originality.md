# Sources and originality

## Intellectual boundary

Shift Signal is independently written software. Its structure, interface, wording, formulas as implemented, fictional examples, documentation and visual identity were created for this repository.

It does not reproduce lecture slides, speaker notes, classroom cases, assignments, exam material, course diagrams or institution-specific teaching material. General topics such as menu engineering, product cannibalization and difference-in-differences only define the problem domain. The implementation rests on public publications and standard statistical methods.

The restaurant menu (Classic burger, Smoky mushroom burger, …) and coffee portfolio (Everyday 250 g, Organic dark roast 250 g, …) examples, their locations, volumes, prices and costs are generated by code and wholly fictional. They are not market evidence.

## What the sources support

The same list ships inside the app (**Research & limits**) and in every evidence pack.

- **Noone and Cachia (2020)** study substitution between menu items in 48 steakhouse outlets using own- and cross-price elasticities. Shift Signal takes the idea that a menu change should be judged across the whole comparable set. It does not reproduce or estimate their elasticity model.
- **van Heerde, Srinivasan and Dekimpe (2010)** show that new-product sales can come from the firm's own products, competitors or category expansion. Shift Signal separates launch volume from portfolio incrementality, but with own-sales data competitor switching and category growth stay combined.
- **Train (2009)** covers discrete-choice models for estimating substitution between alternatives. Shift Signal's overlap weights are user assumptions, not fitted preferences; a choice experiment is the route to estimating them.
- **Bertrand, Duflo and Mullainathan (2004)** show that serial correlation can make conventional difference-in-differences standard errors misleading. Shift Signal resamples whole locations. A bootstrap over few locations is still fragile and does not repair a weak design.
- **Brodersen et al. (2015) and the CausalImpact documentation** state that controls must be unaffected by the intervention and that the pre-period relationship must stay informative afterwards. Shift Signal uses a simple two-group comparison, not the Bayesian structural time-series model, but applies the same checks on control contamination and concurrent changes.

No source validates the planner's proportional allocation rule or the app's warning thresholds (4 and 10 locations, 4 periods). Those are transparent design choices.
